Brain Enter Expected Text Inheritance Report

Date: 2026-06-07

Goal

增强微信回复连续动作的鲁棒性：当 Brain 在批准输入草稿后的下一步返回 key_press enter 但漏带 expected_text 时，backend 仍能把刚刚输入的草稿带到 Enter proposal 中，用于发送后验证。

Worker Split

  • brain: 继续维持一轮只提出一个最小动作的决策边界。
  • human-ops: 在审批链路中继承上一条已执行 type_text 的文本到下一条 Enter proposal。
  • qa-reports: 用契约测试覆盖模型漏带 expected_text 的容错路径，并记录本报告。

  • backend/app.py

Achieved Effect

  • Post-approval continuation now detects the pattern: previous approved action is type_text, next proposal is Enter, and Enter has no expected_text.
  • In that pattern, backend copies the executed or proposed draft text into the Enter proposal before emitting the next Human Ops approval request.
  • The Enter verification prompt can therefore still ask Observe to check whether the exact reply appears in WeChat chat history, even when the model omitted the field.
