Backend Human Ops Decision Route Split

Date: 2026-06-15

Goal

Move the Human Ops proposal decision route orchestration out of backend/app.py while preserving the external SSE and HTTP behavior.

Worker Split

This worker owned the backend Human Ops decision route split. Other active workers own desktop shell, frontend, body observation, settings, and adjacent backend context splits; their existing worktree changes were left intact.

  • Proposal record status semantics remain rejected, approved, executed, and failed.
  • SSE event names and payload fields for meta, display_segment, done, phase, and approval_required are preserved by regression coverage.
