Brain Click Coordinate Guard Report

Date: 2026-06-07

Goal

防止 Brain 在真实任务中返回缺少 x/y 的 click proposal，导致 Human Ops 给用户展示错误的红点审批。点击是简单人类动作，但它必须有完整、可解析的屏幕坐标。

Worker Split

  • brain: 继续负责把目标推进到单步 click proposal。
  • human-ops: backend 在审批前校验 click 参数，缺少完整坐标时无副作用纠正 Brain。
  • qa-reports: 增加初始 chat stream 和 post-approval continuation 两个路径的缺坐标 click 合同测试。

  • tests/test_neo_backend_contract.py

Achieved Effect

  • click is now considered reviewable only when both x and y are explicitly present and numeric.
  • Initial Brain decisions with incomplete click coordinates are corrected before Human Ops emits an approval request.
  • Post-approval continuation applies the same guard, so a multi-step WeChat flow cannot leak an incomplete click after the first approved action.
